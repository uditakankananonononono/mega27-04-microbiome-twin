2. Problem statements and notation
2.1 Assemblage-to-composition (cNODE task)
Let N be the species pool. A sample is a binary assemblage z in {0,1}^N and an observed
steady-state composition p on the simplex with supp(p) contained in supp(z). A twin is a
map phi: z -> p-hat. Error is Bray-Curtis dissimilarity:
             BC(p, q) = sum_i |p_i - q_i| / sum_i (p_i + q_i)              (1)

cNODE (Michel-Mata et al., 2022) defines phi(z) = x(1), with x solving the replicator-
type ODE
          dx/dt = x ⊙ ( f(x) - 1 x^T f(x) ), f(x) = W x, x(0) = z / |z|          (2)

so that sum_i x_i is conserved and absent taxa stay at zero. Our presence-only null is
  p-hat_i(z) = z_i m_i / sum_j z_j m_j, m_i = (1/|T|) sum_{s in T} p_{s,i}             (3)

where T is the training set. It has no interaction parameters; its only parameters are the N
training means. Our gLV replicator baseline integrates
          dx/dt = x ⊙ ( r + A x - phi(x) 1 ), phi(x) = x^T (r + A x)             (4)

to a fixed horizon, and GraphTwin applies attention message passing over present taxa:
    m_i = sum_{j in S, j != i} alpha_ij g_ij V h_j, alpha_ij = softmax_j( q_i^T k_j /
               sqrt(d) ), g_ij = sigma( e_i^T U e_j )                (5)

followed by a residual update h_i <- LayerNorm(h_i + MLP([h_i, m_i])) and a masked
softmax over S = supp(z) added to the log-prior log m_i.

2.2 Held-out-subject forecasting (MDSINE2 task)
For subject s, taxon i and time t, let x_{s,i}(t) be absolute abundance (relative abundance
times total 16S qPCR load). A forecaster sees the training subjects' full trajectories, the
perturbation schedule and the held-out subject's initial state, and predicts x-hat_{s,i}(t)
for all t. The MDSINE2 paper's evaluation (paper_figures/fig3_cross_validation.ipynb)
computes, per (subject, taxon),
 RMSE_{s,i} = sqrt( (1/|D_{s,i}|) sum_{t in D_{s,i}} ( log10(x_{s,i}(t) + eps) - log10(x-
       hat_{s,i}(t) + eps) )^2 ), D_{s,i} = { t : x_{s,i}(t) > 1e-5 }        (6)

with eps = 1e3 in the notebook's units, and reports the distribution of RMSE_{s,i} over
all pairs. Note the set D_{s,i}: only timepoints with detected truth are scored. Our
presence-conditional population forecaster is
mu_i(t) = mean_{o in O : x_{o,i}(t) > 0} log10 x_{o,i}(t), log10 x-hat_{s,i}(t) = mu_i(t)
                   + w(t) [ log10 x_{s,i}(t0) - mu_i(t0) ]       (7)

     w(t) = exp( -(t - t0) / tau ), tau chosen by inner leave-one-subject-out over O
                                                   (8)

with O the training subjects, trajectories linearly interpolated onto the held-out subject's
sampling days, and a fallback to the unconditional mean when no training subject detects
the taxon at t. Paired comparisons use the two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test over (s, i)
pairs:
       W = sum_k sgn(d_k) R_k, d_k = RMSE^ours_k - RMSE^other_k                       (9)

and bootstrap intervals for the null's median use 5000 resamples of the leave-one-out
error vector:
CI_95 = [ Q_0.025, Q_0.975 ] of { median( e*_b ) }_{b=1..5000}, e*_b ~ resample(e)
