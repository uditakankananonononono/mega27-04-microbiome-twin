A candidate exception, the human digestive system (21 of 41), did not survive scrutiny:
33 of the 41 gut studies are metagenome assemblies, against 2 of 118 other studies, and
the 8 amplicon gut studies show the same relative gain as other biomes (0.210 vs 0.184).
Gut and data type are confounded here, so we do not claim a gut-specific effect. This
larger audit also means the cNODE null result (Section 5.4) should be read narrowly: on
these two cNODE tables a mean-composition null matched published cNODE, but across
160 amplicon and assembly studies interaction structure usually does carry predictive
information.
