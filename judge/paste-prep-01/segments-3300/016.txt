5.6.1 Physiological traits of keystones: the anaerobe-keystone candidate
We joined each genus to the Madin et al. (2020) trait database (14,893 species;
condensed_species_NCBI.csv), aggregating species to genus as the share of strict
anaerobes, Gram-negative, motile and sporulating species, and the median genome size,
GC content and doubling time. We test each trait against keystone frequency (Spearman,
BH across traits) and top-25 membership (Mann-Whitney).
