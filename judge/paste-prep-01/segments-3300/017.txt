Genomic replication (KEGG). To avoid relying on one phenotype database, we scored
marker genes across 11,951 KEGG genomes (203 of 248 genera mapped):
pyruvate:ferredoxin oxidoreductase (PFOR; K00169 or K03737), [FeFe]-hydrogenase,
complete dissimilatory sulfate reduction (dsrA, dsrB and aprA), aa3 cytochrome c
oxidase (coxA, K02274), catalase and cytochrome bd. The genomic anaerobe index is
 GAI_j = (1/|g_j|) sum_{x in g_j} 1[PFOR in x] - (1/|g_j|) sum_{x in g_j} 1[coxA in x]
                                                (21)

It agrees with the Madin anaerobe share (Spearman 0.84) and tracks keystone frequency
(rho = 0.36, p = 9e-08). In the within-study fixed-effect logit with abundance and
prevalence (13,294 rows, 159 studies), GAI has coefficient 0.32 (p = 7e-08). Most of this
is the absence of aerobic respiration (coxA -0.52, p = 6e-06; PFOR alone p = 0.08), plus
complete sulfate reduction (+0.70, p = 3e-05).
Third source (BV-BRC). Oxygen-requirement annotations of 11,323 BV-BRC genomes
cover 102 keystone genera (>= 3 annotated genomes). Their anaerobe share agrees with
Madin (rho = 0.83) and KEGG GAI (rho = 0.81), tracks keystone frequency (rho = 0.34,
p = 6e-04), and keeps its effect in the fixed-effect logit (coefficient 0.44, p = 6e-04; 6,804
rows, 151 studies). The three trait sources overlap partly in their curated inputs, so they
are not fully independent.
