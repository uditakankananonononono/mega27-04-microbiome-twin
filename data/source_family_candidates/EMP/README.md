## 14:58 IST taxonomy-name correction

An audit of the fixed BIOM's seven-level taxonomy found 32 genus strings used with more than one parental lineage. The earlier `emp_2k_candidate_genus.tsv.gz` sums those strings together and is retained as an archived *diagnostic*, not a clean taxonomy crosswalk. For example, `Clostridium` occurs under five distinct higher-rank paths in the BIOM. This affects the already-ineligible three-study debug pilot, where median ambiguous-name mass *within named-genus mass* was 6.51%, 3.62%, and 2.28% for studies 804, 1642, and 933, respectively; one sample in 804 was 93.86%. This does not rehabilitate or interpret its model errors.

`scripts/emp_unambiguous_genus.py` now builds a separate conservative candidate table `emp_2k_unambiguous_genus.tsv.gz` from the checksum-verified BIOM. It discards all features with ambiguous genus strings, as well as unnamed genera, and records exclusions. The table has 1,626 genus strings x 1,132 sample columns, no zero-retained sample, and output checksum in its `*_info.json`. Its median *raw BIOM* count mass retained across these candidate samples is only 32.06%, minimum 0.21%; this denominator includes all unnamed genus features. These low values prohibit a general full-community genus forecast claim. Taxonomic synonyms across reference databases and source-mirror independence still require resolution. The old pilot may not be retroactively rescored as an untouched test.

## 23:12 IST internal development baseline on existing EMP subset
`scripts/emp_within_study_baseline.py` checks the committed unambiguous-genus derivative against its pinned SHA-256 and joins 2k metadata by exact sample ID. It leaves each `host_subject_id` out **within its own study**, trains a population presence-mean on the other labels and reports the held-out sample Bray-Curtis on the retained named-unambiguous genus subset. Of 20 metadata candidates, 16 have at least ten distinct host labels and were scored: 803 sample columns and 614 study-specific host-ID labels, **not** 614 verified independent persons. Four studies abstain under this exploratory ten-label screen: 1622 (36 rows, one host label), 1748 (28, five), 1883 (106, one), 2382 (159, six). Median across the 16 studies of their mean host-label-median Bray-Curtis is **0.56805** (study values range 0.29739-0.79592). Per-study counts/scores and metadata/table hashes are in `results/emp_within_study_baseline.json`.

This is a concrete error calculation on already viewed data, **not** cross-source transfer: test host labels share their study with training labels, no eligible top comparator was run, host IDs may be environmental sampling objects, taxonomy mapping discards a median 67.94% of raw BIOM mass, and source-family duplication beyond exact EBI accession remains unresolved. The 804/1642/933 studies were viewed during an earlier failed debug pilot, so these within-study scores never become untouched final evidence.
