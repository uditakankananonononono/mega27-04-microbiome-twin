# Antibiotic/probiotic-adjunct study: header-only source lead

Original 2024 randomized, placebo-controlled study: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiomes/articles/10.3389/frmbi.2024.1359580/full . Its paper reports 50 adults (25 probiotic adjunct, 25 placebo), all prescribed antibiotics for non-gastrointestinal conditions, with baseline, immediately post-antibiotic and day-30 stool collections. ENA project explicitly cited in the paper: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJEB71357 . Exact run metadata retrieval (study-specific public ENA endpoint): https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=PRJEB71357&result=read_run&fields=run_accession,sample_accession,sample_title,study_accession,library_strategy&format=tsv . Downloaded TSV SHA-256 `348cccfc34bb2873a76445c2141c9499ea16d3e193c22ee570ba3a4fc4a82d2a` on 2026-09-26; the query's live output can change and should be re-hashed before claims. Only aggregate result and script are committed; sample IDs and raw reads are not.

`python scripts/prjeb71357_design_screen.py /path/to/filereport.tsv` verifies bytes and checks the five metadata columns, uniqueness and title shape. It has not read sequences, taxon profiles, outcomes, patient traits or trial arm labels. Result: 145 WGA runs/145 sample accessions, 50 person-like F-number prefixes; title suffixes BL 49, MP 48, EP 48; 45 prefixes have all three observed title suffixes and 47 have BL/EP. A published corrigendum https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiomes/articles/10.3389/frmbi.2024.1484878/full explicitly defines BL as baseline, PA as post-antibiotic and EP as endpoint. The deposited **MP** suffix is not PA and its exact meaning is not defined by the paper; do not call its 48 occurrences verified post-antibiotic samples. Five prefixes lack at least one phase. Arm assignments cannot be recovered safely from sample titles, and the exact trial subject map and missing samples need original study confirmation.

This is an adjunct-treatment versus placebo comparison during varied antibiotics, not the same task as single-antibiotic recovery in CEREMI or adult transplant-course observational ARMORD. Do not treat it as a qualifying final study yet: independence from all old audit mirrors, assay/profile compatibility, data-use terms, arm linkage, protocol-known inputs, subject-independent split, benchmark comparator and outcome definition are open. Its design metadata has been viewed, so if it is adapted to, it cannot later be presented as an untouched final holdout. No prediction, benchmark beat, discovery or score.
