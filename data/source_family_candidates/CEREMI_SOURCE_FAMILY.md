# CEREMI source-family guard, 2026-09-26 18:08 IST

This is a **publication/accession overlap warning**, not an ingested or scored cohort. The 2019 16S trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6535507/ reports 22 healthy volunteers receiving ceftriaxone or cefotaxime for 3 days. The 2024 longitudinal multi-omics report https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40168-023-01746-0 reports the same CEREMI 22-volunteer treatment design with 12 fecal samples per volunteer up to 90 days. The 2022 ecological-dynamics paper https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/en/publications/modeling-the-bacterial-dynamics-in-the-gut-microbiota-following-a/ explicitly says it models CEREMI trial 16S plus absolute bacterial counts using generalized Lotka-Volterra and nonlinear mixed effects. The 2024 report's ENA shotgun accession PRJEB58157 appears in its data-availability statement; the earlier 16S publication is indexed under PRJEB28341 at https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJEB28341/ . Confirm exact run and participant mapping before any merge, but **do not count these accessions or papers as independent patient cohorts**. Do not split modalities from the same trial between training and unseen final test.

The published gLV/mixed-effect analysis is a possible same-task comparator, subject to reimplementation/access, matched predictor availability, taxonomic resolution, absolute-vs-relative units, horizon, split and metric. It has not been run here. No source files or outcomes have been read and neither accession is certified as a final study. Data-use and intervention labels remain unverified.
