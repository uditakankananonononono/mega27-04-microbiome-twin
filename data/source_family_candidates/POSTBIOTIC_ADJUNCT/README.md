# Postbiotic/placebo antibiotic adjunct trial - rights-gated source lead

Original pilot randomized trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12797998/ reports V4 16S profiling of 157 samples during a postbiotic-versus-placebo adjunct to prescribed antibiotics. Its own data-availability statement says processed 16S results and code are available as supplemental material (Table S2) and raw sequencing reads at BioProject PRJNA1032616 https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1032616 . The article's supplemental-material notice states ASM does not own copyright in supplemental files and directs readers to the corresponding author for reuse. Do not assume the article CC-BY license transfers to Table S2 or other supplementary material. No author contacted, and the supplemental outcome files have not been opened.
