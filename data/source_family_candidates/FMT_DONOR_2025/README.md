# 2025 FMT stool-donor antibiotic series - screened lead, not a benchmark

Original article: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2025.1589704/full . Original NCBI BioProject: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1202717 . Article-linked analysis code: https://github.com/ramin-karimian/fmt_pj ; inspected default commit via https://api.github.com/repos/ramin-karimian/fmt_pj/commits/main (`0da4dfb71bcdf088df97d779d590b4033a4c6abd`, 2025-01-04). These URLs were read live on 2026-09-27; this source has not been downloaded or modeled here.

The paper reports 61 longitudinal stool samples from **three donors**, split 34/19/8, with different antibiotics, durations and collection windows. NCBI BioProject lists 61 BioSamples and 61 SRA experiments, 83,805 MB sequence data. The paper's data-availability statement gives PRJNA1202717 and the author's analysis-code repository, which contains MetaPhlAn/Kraken table-creation scripts but no checked-in abundance/subject/day matrix at its top level. Three donors are three biological units, not 61 independent people or a robust held-out-source statistical benchmark. Donor 3 underwent two courses; date and course-window reconstruction is required before any recovery forecast. The paper describes its MetaPhlAn3 abundance postprocessing, including prevalence and low-abundance filters; a new downstream profile must replicate or explicitly differ from that pipeline.

It is a separate 2025 shotgun research lead, not one of the archived old-MGnify accession IDs by exact text search in `data/raw/mgnify/manifest.csv`. That exact miss cannot prove absence of sample mirrors or independent patient cohorts. Sequence processing would be about 84 GB before additional processing, so the free-first feasibility gate is open. Study-specific data-use rights, exact subject/time/antibiotic labels per BioSample, cross-source taxonomic harmonization, a same-task comparator, adequate independent donor count and an untouched final protocol are also open. No patient-level trial-arm outcome, prediction error, causal response or discovery is established by this metadata screen. Do not count this as a validated external dataset or a foundation-scale addition.
