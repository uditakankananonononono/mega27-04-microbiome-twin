## Source validation update, 2026-09-26 14:24 IST
- HMP official data model https://www.hmpdacc.org/hmp/overview/data-model/ confirms a 300-person healthy cohort, many subsites and repeated visits, and says public clinical metadata are restricted to subject ID, body site, sex and visit number; richer clinical data require dbGaP access. Do not extract/re-identify controlled clinical metadata. HMP16SData V13 https://waldronlab.io/HMP16SData/reference/V13.html is a processed 2,898-sample V1-3 OTU table with `RSID` person IDs and 16S taxon `GENUS` annotation; V35 is separately processed and would not be an independent cohort. No HMP bytes or benchmark result downloaded here.
- AGP fixed manuscript source https://ftp.microbio.me/AmericanGut/manuscript-package/readme.txt explicitly selects one fecal sample per participant. Our fixed BIOM has 9,511 sample/unique host IDs, but old MGnify manifest includes PRJEB11419 American Gut assembly MGYS00006069. Thus it is quarantined for cross-mirror overlap, not an external holdout result.
- EMP fixed Zenodo v1 https://zenodo.org/records/890000 contains 125 cataloged study IDs, 97 marked release1; the 2k sample metadata subset spans 95 IDs, and lacks host_subject_id on 127 rows. Its catalog lists AGP Qiita 10317 but marks it *not* release1, and our subset has no AGP rows. Study-level mirror overlap with MGnify still needs accession matching. Data details and checksums in `data/source_family_candidates/EMP/README.md`; no observation table/model scores yet.
