# Candidate antibiotic-recovery prediction track, metadata pre-registration draft, 2026-09-26 16:42 IST

**Status: not frozen for outcome scoring.** This is a candidate design following failure of the broad composition-transfer gate. Sources, units, rights, processed tables and usable follow-up windows must be reconciled before a locked analysis plan. It must not be used to claim that recovery prediction has been run or that an external test source is untouched. The aim remains a research-use microbiome twin platform with uncertainty and explanations, not treatment advice.

**Proposed prediction task:** Given a taxonomically valid baseline stool profile, protocol-known treatment class and a fixed post-treatment horizon, predict later community state and recovery relative to that person's baseline. Inputs cannot include the person's post-treatment profile. Evaluate profiles and recovery separately, including a transparent persistence baseline and a train-only nearest-neighbor baseline. Outcome definition, compositional scale, time tolerance and handling missing baseline genus mass must be fixed *before opening outcomes* on any designated final study.

**Development sources:** Hagan 2019 has two phases with 33 subject labels but requires exact phase/assay/sample mapping. Harris 2018 has a subject-arm identity conflict and cannot be used without source reconciliation. The NUH study has 24 subjects and complete PRE/DURING/POST metadata but its table has redundant higher-rank taxon columns and needs data-use verification; it can be development only after this metadata inspection. Palleja has 114 runs/57 sample accessions and its inspected accession metadata lacks person/day; original mappings required. Taur mixes 16S/WGS and treatment provenance remains unresolved. MDSINE2 small mouse cohorts can be a separate absolute-abundance task, not pooled numerically with human relative-composition data. Do not use EMP 804/1642/933, HMP subsites or the scored oral MGnify candidate as new untouched finals after they have been viewed.

**Validation plan:** reserve at least one **new** study family whose taxonomic and assay method matches the development task and whose subjects, runs and mirrors cannot overlap training or tuning. Study/subject holdout with no shared runs or aliquots. Train-only harmonization, fixed availability of treatment labels at forecast time, timepoint windows and missing-data policy. Source-level macro metrics and subject-aware uncertainty; a single eligible final study remains insufficient for a general cross-source win. The strongest same-task comparator needs a published or established implementation on the same data, predictor inputs, split and metric; a simple model can win. Family-wise multiplicity and negative arms reported. Any failure is recorded, prompts a new preregistered pivot and does not convert this test into an untouched test again.

**Outcome status:** no model trained on new recovery data, no locked external test, no benchmark beat or biological discovery. The prospective question that could be falsified is whether a pre-treatment ecological state plus a fixed exposure label predicts post-treatment resilience beyond persistence/nearest-neighbor and eligible published dynamics tools on disjoint subjects/studies. This is a hypothesis, not a conclusion. The older oral prior lead and failed EMP/HMP coverage are reasons to test it, not evidence it holds.

## 20:20 IST course-window design guard (metadata only)
`microtwin.course_design.screen_courses` checks that a course has a strictly before-start baseline and strictly after-end follow-up, names source family and subject, and flags another course intersecting the chosen closest-sample window. It is synthetic-tested; no real cohort was fed to it, and it does not establish exposure validity or effect. This prevents repeated event rows (notably DIABIMMUNE and ARMORD) from being mistaken for independent, unambiguous interventions. If a source lacks start/end, subject, arm or sample time, the analysis remains blocked instead of inventing a mapping. Patient time, assay comparability, rights and final untouched source are separate gates.
