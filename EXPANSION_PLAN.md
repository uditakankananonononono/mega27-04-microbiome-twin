# Microbiome twin platform expansion, started 2026-09-26

Source of scope: Udita's September 26, 1:36 PM IST WhatsApp request. This document is a plan and gate ledger, not a claim that the expansion is finished. The original v3 audit paper stays an archived baseline; it is not the final expanded platform paper.

## Baseline and honest counts
- VERIFIED EXISTING: six cNODE ecosystem tables and matched-fold base results; 160 MGnify study audits across eight biomes; two MDSINE2 small mouse cohorts; `microtwin audit` and the population forecast CLI. See v3 paper and `results/` for protocols and caveats.
- THIN: current MGnify study-level signal is association/prediction, not causal interaction necessity; mixed amplicon and assembly studies confound biome claims. A six-dataset local stack tie does not imply unseen-source transfer or a top-tool beat.
- MISSING FOR NEW SCOPE: independent external source-transfer leaderboard, 500-1000 QC-passed study manifest, trustworthy intervention forecasts, calibrated twin reliability, paired multimodal datasets, foundation-scale pretraining, independent keystone-under-change discovery, a general-user research platform.

## Frozen gate design before new results
G0. Verify immutable source accessions, sampling unit, modality, license, source hashes, deduplication, taxonomy, labels, eligible tasks, and train/validation/test manifest. Entire unseen studies and source families held out. No patient/subject overlap or near duplicate across splits. Train-only preprocessing.
G1. Reproduce original baseline scores and unit tests in an isolated environment, recording code SHA, data IDs, split seeds and all failures.
G2. Introduce a *predictive* interaction-dependence score. For a held-out sample with known truth, `(prior_error - interaction_error)/max(prior_error, eps)` gives signed fractional improvement; an ecosystem summary is a median with a study-/subject-aware interval. This does not identify causal ecological interactions. A new sample without an outcome gets a score only via a selector trained/calibrated without its source; otherwise report `unavailable`, not an invented held-out gain.
G3. True source transfer: train on one source, tune on disjoint studies, test on unseen MGnify, HMP, American Gut and Earth Microbiome data when eligible. Report failed harmonizations and out-of-distribution studies.
G4. Two honest leaderboard tracks: assemblage-to-composition (population prior, cNODE, gLV, GNN, transformer) and longitudinal absolute-abundance/intervention (population, gLV, MDSINE2 and compatible models). Different data/metrics cannot be ranked in one numeric table. Target 500-1000 independently accessioned, QC-passed datasets before claiming that scale. Match folds, information, compute and metric. Freeze primary external-study macro-median Bray-Curtis difference vs the strongest same-task eligible baseline; paired study-level confidence interval must exclude zero for a win. Secondary longitudinal log10-RMSE and perturbation direction accuracy are reported separately, with multiplicity and failed runs.
G5. Validate simulated species removal, antibiotic and diet direction against independent perturbation experiments; held-out interventions, negative controls and uncertainty, not observational network edges as ground truth.
G6. Twin Reliability Index candidate: separately show held-out accuracy, predictive interaction gain, calibration/uncertainty, transfer loss and robustness. A 0-100 composite requires preregistered weights and calibration on training sources, untouched-source validation of coverage vs empirical error and abstention. No arbitrary confidence score.
G7. Pretrain across biomes only after sourced millions-of-samples feasibility, compute budget and input harmonization. Report actual studies/samples and resources; do not count projected scale as verified.
G8. Paired 16S/metagenomic/metabolomic/pathway study ablations with missing-modality controls and subject/source holdouts. Do not treat unrelated modality cohorts as paired.
G9. Predict *future* keystones under measured environmental change using temporal/intervention outcomes and interpretable features. Pre-register operational labels and independent validation; no inferred-network hub treated as validated keystone.
G10. Research-use modular platform: versioned table ingestion, schema and consent checks for patient data, provenance, explicit model eligibility/abstention, individual forecasts where warranted, intervals, explanations and reproducible exports. No clinical advice or treatment recommendation.

Discovery bar: pre-register one falsifiable interaction-vs-prior transfer/perturbation hypothesis, replicate it independently across held-out source families and modality strata, and retain negative results. Benchmarks must win on untouched data to claim a beat; there is no such claim yet. Free-first compute; scale jobs only after measurable feasibility and cost gate.

## First metadata screen (2026-09-26)
`results/expansion_manifest_screen.json`, generated by `microtwin.eligibility.assess_records`, screens the existing 160-row MGnify manifest. It contains 160 unique MGYS IDs and 160 source URLs. Of those, 127 are metadata candidates; 33 require review because their study names indicate a possible metagenome assembly. **Verified independent external datasets = 0** at this stage: no file-byte, sample overlap, subject, modality, license, or held-out source verification has been completed for the new leaderboard. The older 160-study audit remains an audit, not automatically 160 eligible source-transfer cohorts. The counts are metadata-only and may change on closer validation.

First new MGnify candidate: `data/external_candidate/MGYS00005779.md` records a 64-sample study whose taxonomy download contains 126 analysis columns (64 runs + 62 assemblies of the same study's samples). Do not count 126 as independent samples or use it in a win claim. It is absent from the prior 160-study manifest but awaits metadata/subject/license and exact split validation. No external test metric has been computed.

Second new MGnify candidate: `data/external_candidate/MGYS00002146.md` records 178 amplicon runs mapped to 87 samples (83 samples with two runs, four with three). The initial single-run selector fails closed on such multiple-run samples. A separate outcome-blind run-summing policy now pools 178 runs to 87 sample IDs, but assay comparability and person-level units still need verification before eligibility. Unscored and not counted toward verified independent external datasets.

Outcome-blind taxonomy gate: `terminal_genus` selects only genus-terminal SSU rows, excluding parent ranks and species children to avoid double-counting reads. On the two pinned new-study candidates this retains 339/930 (oral) and 275/816 (soil) taxonomic rows, but taxonomy version/synonym harmonization is still open. No cross-source metric computed.

Dry-run candidate transformations only (no model fitting): run pooling + terminal-genus selection yields oral 339 genera x 87 sample IDs and soil 275 genera x 64 sample IDs. All columns have nonzero genus mass, but one soil sample has just one detected genus; target-domain quality thresholds must be locked before scoring. The two candidate sets have 89 overlapping genus names, insufficient alone to assert compatible taxonomy or good transfer coverage. Oral sample IDs are not yet subject-independent.

`microtwin.platform_schema.validate_abundance_table` now rejects malformed counts/relative/absolute matrices and requires a processing-consent assertion plus source ID before research-use ingestion; this is schema validation only, not access control, consent verification or a deployed upload platform. `BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md` freezes the new-source win test and `EXTERNAL_SOURCE_LEDGER.md` maps the four requested source families. No external prediction has been evaluated.

A small `TransformerTwin` taxon-token attention baseline is now wired into the existing composition predictor interface and covered by simplex/masking/smoke-fit tests. This is an unbenchmarked model family, **not** the requested pretrained microbiome foundation model or a top-tool win. Its comparison belongs to the frozen external-study protocol; no new-source prediction was read.
