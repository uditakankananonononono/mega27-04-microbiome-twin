# Frozen small-n ciprofloxacin direction check
Written locally on 2026-10-01 before opening abundance cells or computing direction results. Not independently registered. Inputs: original PLOS Dataset S3 V3 refOTUs sheet and primary Table 1. Parent-relayed source scan found no relevant indexed exposure, but binary/archive contents and files over 20MB excluded; listings cannot prove no past opening. Current research has opened primary published findings, table headers and schedule, not abundance values. This is therefore a prospective computation on screened public data, not a certified untouched benchmark.

Limits: 3 subjects, no untreated controls, OTU-level not validated species, same source family as PNAS. Frame: a small-n prospective direction check, not a general antibiotic-response claim. Ciprofloxacin 500 mg twice daily for 5 days. First administration is day 0. Pair each subject's closest pretreatment day -1 (A2c/B2/C2) with day +5 (A3b/B3/C3); do not pool A day +3 or long-term recovery days. These observations support relative-abundance change under exposure, not absolute biomass or a causal contrast against no drug.

Freeze all 5670 supplied refOTUs without outcome-driven selection. Convert finite nonnegative per-sample columns to proportions by their column sum; fail rather than impute malformed cells or missing taxa. Leave one entire subject out. Predict held-out direction using (a) species-free OTU-wise median delta of the other two subjects and (b) delta of the nearest pretreatment donor (Bray-Curtis; subject-label tie break). These are fixed transparent baselines, not novel algorithms and not the platform interaction model. No tuning or model training; no benchmark beat or discovery gate credited from this test.

Primary endpoint: subject-macro direction accuracy among held-out OTUs with observed absolute change >=0.001 (0.1 percentage point), counting zero predictions as incorrect. Threshold defines retrospective evaluation, not predictor eligibility. Report each subject, eligible count, predicted-nonzero coverage and the paired subject-macro difference. Also report a decline-only predictor as a simple ecological directional reference. No asymptotic p-values or bootstrap confidence interval at n=3. Report observed differences only; no significance/generalization claim. Secondary report conditional nonzero accuracy separately, no post-result threshold changes. POST values, published response annotations and held-out DURING values never enter prediction. Taxonomy used only to identify OTU level, not select taxa by published findings.
