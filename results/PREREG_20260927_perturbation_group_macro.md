# Group-macro perturbation direction amendment, 2026-09-27 21:33 IST

The current synthetic-only `direction_accuracy` weights every taxon change equally. A single large experiment may then dominate an intervention metric, so add a separate group-macro accuracy, defined as the mean of within-group attempted-direction accuracies. Also report group-level eligible, attempted and coverage counts, while suppressing group identifiers in the result (only aggregate group size/accuracy records). For uncertainty, resample *experiment groups* with replacement at a fixed seed, not individual taxa; report a percentile 95% interval on the group-macro accuracy only when at least two attempted experiment groups exist, otherwise leave it unavailable. Freeze `n_boot=2000` and `seed=0` defaults, with validated bounded integer controls. Existing pooled accuracy remains for backward compatibility and no new real perturbation dataset is introduced. An asserted group label is not proof of independence, and neither nominal coverage nor interval supports causality, patient-specific advice or a benchmark beat. Test imbalanced groups, zero predictions, threshold edge, one group, invalid controls and reproducibility. Do not alter historical audit metrics.
