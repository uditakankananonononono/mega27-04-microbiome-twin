## Target and prior art

Operational goal: emit an explicit pair of stable gLV systems matching one observed equilibrium yet predicting opposite *target* abundance changes after permanent removal of another species, or abstain when the specified bounded family cannot furnish such a pair. This is a demonstration of an inference limit, **not** an empirical microbiome intervention predictor or a proof about a patient's true ecology. Structural-identifiability problems are known: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10135947/ .
