## Minimum independent evidence for a prospective ecological-keystone claim

The term 'keystone' needs an operational ecological endpoint, not network centrality alone. Proposed endpoint for a separately reviewed experiment/design: after a controlled removal/addition of a named organism, measure a predeclared community-composition or functional change at a fixed horizon relative to an explicit matched control. Species detection, baseline abundance, community size, intervention delivery and successful manipulation must be measured. Relative-abundance changes alone can reflect denominator shifts; functional or absolute outcomes require their own calibrated assay contracts.

Before any new response data are opened, freeze:
1. One primary biological hypothesis and one endpoint/horizon. Record prior-art differences, exact taxa/traits and exclusion rules. This map does not establish novelty.
2. Recruitment/source-family independence and actual randomized unit (donor, culture, cage or participant). Technical wells and repeated timepoints cannot inflate independent n. Independent discovery and replication cohorts must be separate recruited/source units, not different representations of the same study.
3. Source rights, processed baseline and target data, person/specimen/aliquot/time/intervention dictionary, assay units/taxonomy/QC and matching control. Unknown load-bearing fields stop admission. No clinical recommendation follows.
4. Locked scoring methods, training-only preprocessing, feature vocabulary and all comparators. Candidate ranking must depend on baseline data only. No response-derived network, target-conditioned taxon filtering or publication-selected responders. Viewed sources stay exposed development.
5. Biological-unit macro loss/ranking score, coverage/abstention and uncertainty resampling at the actual independent unit. For keystone ranking, report precision at a fixed k alongside a prevalence/abundance-matched null and full ranking quality; choose k from design capacity before outcomes. Sample size/precision planning must use external or declared planning assumptions, not a favorable effect in the final source.
6. Useful-win threshold, multiplicity and failure rules. A primary effect and independent replication both need their predeclared directional/effect-size/uncertainty criteria; no threshold relaxation, favorable metric swap or post-failure redirect is called confirmation.

Minimum fair baseline set: intervention-blind/persistence response expectation where defined; training-only donor or community response mean; abundance/prevalence-only ranking; a strongest eligible same-task published method verified before scoring. A model given only presence cannot be compared as if it received the same longitudinal inputs as a dynamic model. If no eligible leading method exists, the top-tool goal remains unresolved, not waived.

An association-only independent cohort could test a trait-to-inferred-hub hypothesis. It would NOT validate ecological necessity or a personalized perturbation twin. A successful removal/addition endpoint could validate prospective response ranking within its experimental domain; it would NOT automatically validate host clinical outcomes, other interventions or a universal reliability index.

## Separate measurement-pattern route

Blautia is currently a better-defined observable than a causal keystone candidate, but it addresses measurement reliability, not the user's full discovery goal by itself. A later separately reviewed documentary unit could resolve RR-6 identity/recruitment and prior-art specificity without fitting. A later empirical route would require same physical specimen/aliquot, full taxonomy/version map, a predeclared denominator and comparator, extraction/library batch controls, and an orthogonal quantification method. Agreement under common processing would argue for pipeline/reference effects; persistent discrepancy with orthogonal evidence would identify which assay estimate is closer to the target. Neither result alone proves an ecological mechanism.

No NASA downloads, reruns, RR-8 activity or source hunt are initiated by naming this route. Existing exposed pairs cannot be repackaged as untouched tests. RR-5/RR-6 different missions are not yet two certified comparable biological experiments.
