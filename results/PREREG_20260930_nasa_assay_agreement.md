# Bounded exploratory NASA RR-5 assay agreement analysis
Frozen before first processed table read: 2026-09-30 09:40 IST.
Purpose: quantify paired genus-level disagreement between NASA processed V4, V1V3 and MetaPhlAn assays, not invent a new algorithm or claim a replicated biological discovery. Use exactly matched specimen IDs, then inspect ISA metadata for mouse/material/timepoint reconciliation. No flight inference until group, repeated-measure and cage mapping is established. Initial estimands: per-specimen Bray-Curtis distance and genus-level median abundance differences across paired assays. Collapse explicit genus annotations only; report unclassified mass and shared/assay-specific taxa. Use paired Wilcoxon with BH across all eligible shared genera (nonzero in at least 20% of paired specimens in either assay), plus effect sizes; retain zeros. Taxonomic synonym/version mismatches and measurement biases can explain differences. If pairs do not match, report that and amend without calling it a negative biology finding. All opened specimens become exposed exploratory data, not an untouched holdout. Download only README, small processed TSVs and ISA metadata, capped at 20 MB each. No raw reads, alignments or model runs.
