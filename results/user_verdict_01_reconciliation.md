## Two-part scoring, metric compatibility caveat

A post-verdict protocol (`results/PREREG_20260927_mdsine_dual_endpoints.md`) decomposes the **already viewed** released mouse-cohort predictions into a direct positive-vs-zero threshold at the source's truth cutoff 1e-5 and log-abundance RMSE conditional on truth detection. The calculation is in `scripts/mdsine_dual_endpoints.py`; its conditional pair medians exactly reproduce the archived official values. At this threshold MDSINE2 without modules predicts positive almost everywhere: 8 true negatives out of 19,535 truth-negative timepoints in healthy and 5 of 18,449 in UC, with 100% positive sensitivity; the simple baseline has sensitivities 95.1%/97.1% and specificities 63.2%/58.1%. This is **not** a fair detection benchmark: the published methods supply continuous absolute-abundance forecasts, not calibrated detection probabilities, and this cutoff was designed for truth scoring, not necessarily predictions in the same units. It is a diagnostic of endpoint mismatch and why a new dual-metric standard needs model-specific threshold calibration on training data before independent testing. The conditional-abundance RMSE is the original detected-only score, not an independent second experiment. Four and five mice remain the only biological units; there is no new-source performance claim.
