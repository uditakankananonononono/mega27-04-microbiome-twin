# NASA public-data discovery direction: pre-outcome feasibility, 2026-09-29 07:27 IST

## Question and research route

Could a microbiome-to-host phenotype method trained in one rodent spaceflight mission generalize to an independent mission, rather than merely rediscovering the published flight-associated taxa? Candidate concrete target: species-level microbial composition to predict a measured host metabolic or bone phenotype, with a falsifiable assay-disagreement and age/duration sensitivity analysis. **This is only a question, not a selected outcome or an active preregistered analysis.** Before any outcome-table inspection, name an exact phenotype with matched mouse IDs in both missions, freeze taxonomic harmonization, subgroup and missingness rules, published same-task comparators, fit/validation/test missions and metrics. Without that shared phenotype, the scientifically narrower alternate task would be cross-mission transfer of flight-versus-ground classification, controlling age, duration, cage and habitat, not a host-phenotype discovery. Neither is licensed to call an association causal or to claim a new biomarker without independent replication and prior-art audit.

## Before first outcome read

1. Establish whether OSD-417 and OSD-742 contain the *same measured host endpoint*, matched to each mouse's microbiome row. The titles and paper claims do **not** establish a shared per-mouse bone or metabolite endpoint. If none, explicitly amend to a different preregistered task before opening outcomes.
2. Recover ISA sample/assay tables, reconcile RFID, sample material, cage, run, assay, flight/ground, age, duration and return status; use one mouse as the primary independent unit, keep cage and mission clustering visible. Check whether same specimens appear in multiple assays or source mirrors. Do not treat 1,157 files, 260 samples, 160 mission mice or 145 assays as independent datasets.
3. Check public terms, processed table version, taxonomy, depth/zeros, matched control windows and the cost of any processing. Do not download or analyze the 265.05 GB OSD-417 whole study or RR-8 raw FASTQs as a convenience step. Small metadata-only reads can be approved separately from heavy compute.
4. Read published RR-5, RR-6 and any RR-8 analyses for exact claims and comparator methods before selecting target. Freeze source-family split and model/metric only after the task exists. Leave a genuinely untouched mission, not a second assay from the same mice, for final evaluation. Source-family overlap defeats apparent external validation.
5. Discovery bar: a new method must offer a testable capability against an established same-task predictor, and a new biological result must survive separate-source replication with patient/mouse grouping and multiple-testing control. The old 160-study MGnify Wilcoxon/BH audit is unchanged and is not the training cohort by default.

## Decision

**GO for bounded source/identity/processed-file research only. NO-GO for a host-phenotype outcome test today.** The load-bearing blocker is an exact shared per-mouse host outcome and usable RR-8 processed abundance matrix; sample-unit/cage reconciliation is also pending. No outcome values were opened and no benchmark/discovery gate is credited. Parent may approve a scoped heavy raw-read or harmonization plan later if metadata makes a specific test plausible. This is a time-bounded research direction, not an invitation to keep accumulating negative screens.
