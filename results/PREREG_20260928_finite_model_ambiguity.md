## Prior-art and dynamical-scope correction, 2026-09-28 12:10 IST

The 2024 ensemble ecosystem modelling study https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011976 already generates constrained, feasible/stable gLV ensembles and forecasts intervention effects across them. A finite sign-pair witness is an explicit certificate format within that known class of uncertainty analysis, not a novel algorithm on current evidence. Furthermore, local stability of the candidate post-removal equilibrium does not prove the actual removal trajectory from the pre-removal state converges to it. The generated results are **stable-equilibrium contrasts**, not demonstrated intervention trajectories or causal effect directions. Prior JSON keys `target_change` must be read as target post-removal equilibrium minus pre-removal equilibrium, not an empirically observed change.
