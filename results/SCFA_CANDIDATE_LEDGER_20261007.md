## Claim and decision point
The sought measured panel gates a source-grounded, subject-disjoint evaluation of a fixed short-horizon in-vivo diet-to-fecal-SCFA response forecast against fixed chemical baselines. It does NOT alone gate taxonomic dynamics, causal dietary efficacy, clinical recommendations, independent discovery, absolute microbial abundance, a leading taxonomic twin-tool beat or whole-platform validation. New diet/assay/horizon means transfer requires a declared compatible task, not automatic yogurt replication. A matched external panel must pass rights, person/sample/visit/arm linkage, assay/units/missingness and source lineage before a scientific protocol and outcome fitting.

Realistic alternatives: re-scope to the already measured within-source yogurt/oats development forecast and its exact negative useful-win gate; seek a comparable public panel while accepting descriptive-only source census for parked candidates; or request author clarification about a specific missing dictionary/normalization through an email whose final recipient and wording the user must approve. No author email is drafted or sent here. Descriptive-only framing does not establish response prediction, and no new synthetic module substitutes for biological evidence.
