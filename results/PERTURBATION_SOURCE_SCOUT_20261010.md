## Ranked candidates

### 1. OMM12 single-species dropout, Weiss et al. 2023
Primary: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40372-0

Metabolomics deposit MSV000090704: https://massive.ucsd.edu/ProteoSAFe/dataset.jsp?accession=MSV000090704

Publisher source-data archive (HEAD only, no download): https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-023-40372-0/MediaObjects/41467_2023_40372_MOESM3_ESM.zip . Live HEAD: 200, 1,195,857 bytes.

Design: full 12-strain consortium versus each of twelve single-strain dropouts, cultured four days with daily serial dilution in two carbohydrate environments (AF/APF). Absolute abundance measured as normalized 16S copies/ml by strain-specific qPCR. Full community also assessed across five media. Mouse arm: full consortium versus three selected dropouts (E. faecalis, B. caecimuris, B. coccoides), gnotobiotic C57BL/6J mice, terminal sampling 20 days after initial colonization. Full consortium is a real intervention control, not a statistical null.

Metadata n: twelve removal conditions plus full community; dropout culture figure declares N=3 per condition; full-consortium culture N=9 in AF/APF and N=6 in the other media. Mouse captions declare N>=8/group for weight endpoint, N=5/group for histology/metabolomics. These counts are assay-specific and cannot be pooled. Separate cultures, inoculum batches and cages need verification before treating any count as independent n.

Rights: primary article explicitly CC BY 4.0, including article material unless otherwise credited; publisher declares source data supplied with the paper. MassIVE explicitly CC0 1.0. Source ZIP member-specific rights/content and sample linkage still unverified; article and MassIVE rights do not automatically license every external file.

Families it could falsify: strongest direct test of whether a locked inferred-hub or literature-proxy rank predicts measured removal impact, rather than merely network centrality. Tests environment specificity of ridge anaerobe/keystone generalization if there is adequate trait/taxon variation. Blautia dropout offers a biological response endpoint but NOT the NASA 16S-versus-shotgun measurement-bias claim. Oral/sulfate families require checking relevant strain coverage; do not assume it.

Independent-replication read would require: archive path/size inventory, exact qPCR/units/copy-number normalization, species/strain and control/dropout map, culture/inoculum batch independence, mouse/cage/time/specimen map, usable baseline/control predictor inputs and target separation. Each medium is an environment sensitivity, not automatically independent recruitment. A separate recruited/lab/source community is needed for biological replication. Published selection of the three mouse dropouts makes their arm selected validation, not a universal rank screen. No untouched label after this paper read.

### 2. Nasal synthetic communities, Navarro-Diaz/Link repository
Primary public design/data README: https://github.com/marcelo-nd/nasalSynComs

Sequence accession PRJNA1370791: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1370791

Metabolomics MSV000096776: https://massive.ucsd.edu/ProteoSAFe/dataset.jsp?accession=MSV000096776

Design: fifty defined communities varying species/strain composition; communities of 5-10 species, time-course assembly, nutrient-context perturbations and S. aureus inclusion/exclusion/coculture comparisons. Repository describes explicit inocula, timepoint metadata and processed composition/metabolite tables. This is actual experimental composition/nutrient variation, not simulated reads or an annotation database. Absence of a species across distinct starting communities is not automatically a matched single-strain dropout.

Metadata n: fifty communities screened; twenty in temporal/metabolomic panels; five in repeated-community panel; three cocultures in three media per README. BioProject lists 308 SRA experiments and 326 BioSamples, which are NOT independent community or participant counts. Number of independently prepared cultures/batches remains unverified.

Rights: repository visibly GPLv3; verify whether this explicitly covers experimental processed tables, not just code. MassIVE metabolomics explicitly CC0. BioProject public and identity matches nasal synthetic-community study. No raw data download needed for proposed processed route.

Families it could falsify: transport of baseline hub/literature-proxy response ranks to a different body-site experimental ecosystem; environment-conditioned anaerobe hypothesis only if trait/strain contrasts are adequate. Does NOT directly replicate oral HOMD-list enrichment in a nasal-only assemblage or NASA assay bias. Species-list and matched perturbation/control eligibility need admission.

Independent-replication read would require: inspect metadata/inocula structure and license scope without OTU outcomes; confirm exact strain/condition/time/run keys, whether media/species contrasts are matched, actual replicate unit and raw/processed linkage. README names S2 inocula, S6 timepoint metadata, S4/S5/S7/S8 OTU panels, S11/S13 metabolite panels; filenames are leads, not verified content. Confirm no shared recruitment/material with OMM12 or archived sources. Independent lab/body-site is useful but changes domain, so freeze a compatible estimand rather than pool scores.

### 3. hCom1a targeted strain removal, Wang et al. 2023
Primary: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10299816/

Metabolomics MSV000091763: https://massive.ucsd.edu/ProteoSAFe/dataset.jsp?accession=MSV000091763

Design: human-derived defined gut community, C. scindens/C. hylemonae individual and double removals versus full hCom1a, additional targeted combinations; germ-free mouse colonization, community composition and bile-acid/metabolite endpoints. Weekly fecal sampling and terminal intestinal specimens; main dropout figure captions state three weeks, general mouse methods four weeks unless stated, another combination ten days. Preserve experiment-specific timing.

Metadata n: primary captions n=3 mice/group for main single/double dropouts; reproducibility arm three independently constructed inocula with n=3-5 mice/inoculum; humanized/conventional controls separate and not removal replicates. Mice/cages/batches and exact assay eligibility unresolved.

Rights/access: MassIVE raw metabolomics CC0, public. Primary says metagenomic sequencing public SRA with accessions in key resources table, and all reported data available upon request. No exact SRA accession was recovered from the inspected table rendering; no guessed accession supplied. Processed taxonomic/chemical table public access and rights UNVERIFIED. No requests made.

Families it could falsify: directly measures necessity/function for named taxa and provides a narrow independent-lab response check for locked inferred rank/literature proxy. Too few preselected taxa to validate broad anaerobe/sulfate/oral trait laws. Need baseline input feasibility; full hCom1a is a control, but published metabolic niche selection is exposed prior knowledge.

Independent-replication read would require: recover exact public processed table inventory/rights and the SRA map, verify sample/mouse/cage/inoculum/control/terminal-time keys, bile-acid assay units and population, shared strain/material relationship to other Fischbach hCom studies. Metabolite raw archive alone is not an admitted paired processed task; a heavy reprocessing route requires a separate concrete plan.

### 4. Serum perturbation of a 22-species oral community, Li et al. 2026
Primary resolved and read: https://academic.oup.com/ismej/article/20/1/wrag070/8550912

Metabolomics ST004636: https://www.metabolomicsworkbench.org/data/DRCCMetadata.php?StudyID=ST004636

Code README: https://github.com/liluacrobat/SerumDrivenDysbiosis

Author sequence pointer PRJNA1336263: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1336263 . Current BioProject page explicitly says ID is not public. Do not equate the paper's deposit claim with current availability.

Design: 22 subgingival species in microaerophilic chemostats; serum 0%,5%,50% as nutritional perturbation. Deposit methods identify 0->5,5->50 and constant-50 experiments, with run1/run2 independent chemostats for 5->50, steady-state before/after samples. Matched no-transition condition exists, but order/time confounding and replication differ by contrast.

Metadata n: Workbench factor page displays 21 samples, not 21 independent experiments. Article: three steady-state samples per run/condition for metatranscriptomics; different days within a chemostat were treated as independent biological replicates by authors. Our inference must instead preserve run-level repeated-measure grouping. Workbench distinguishes independent chemostat runs; exact total run-level n must be reconciled.

Rights: article CC BY 4.0. Workbench metadata public; assays peak-height units, not absolute fecal SCFA concentrations. Explicit deposit/processed-table license not verified. GitHub is scripts, no license shown by fetched README; code availability is not processed data access. Taxonomic SRA public-access gate currently fails.

Families it could falsify: perturbation-response ranking within an oral ecological domain if baseline/control measurements become admitted. Oral-only species coverage cannot identify HOMD oral-versus-nonoral enrichment, and serum perturbation alone is not species necessity/removal. Useful domain-specific response stress test, not independent confirmation of the general oral-hub law or a host inflammatory mechanism.

Independent-replication read would require: public processed composition/metabolite linkage and rights, reconcile run/sample/time/serum order, separate true runs from repeated days, calibrate peak heights/normalization/QC and taxonomy. ST004636 is not enough to fit taxonomic response while PRJNA1336263 is nonpublic. No sign-in/contact/requests attempted.
