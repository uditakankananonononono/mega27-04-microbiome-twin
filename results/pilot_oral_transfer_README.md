# Oral-to-oral one-study pilot, 2026-09-26

This is a debug transfer pilot under `PILOT_TRANSFER_PROTOCOL.md`, **not** the requested 500-1000-dataset leaderboard or a top-tool beat. All models were trained on the 20-site old MGnify oral accession MGYS00002394 and predicted the disjoint accession MGYS00002146; its 178 runs pooled to 87 sites from 58 people (29 controls; 29 diseased with two sites). All use MGnify v4.1 tables and train-vocabulary genus projection. Zero projected-mass sites excluded: 0. Full per-site and per-person errors, source SHA-256s and protocol hash are in `pilot_oral_transfer.json`. No model was tuned on the candidate study.

Primary descriptive macro-person median Bray-Curtis: population presence-mean **0.58346**; GraphTwin 0.58514; cNODE reimplementation 0.59091; gLV 0.60632; experimental transformer 0.61802. Thus the simple prior is numerically best in this *single study* and none of the tested interaction models establishes a win here. Per-site medians differ; see JSON. The 20 train samples and 58 test people are too small/narrow for a global model-choice rule, and a one-study test has no valid study-level interval. Biological study and preprocessing differences can explain errors; the model does not show causal dependence. No MDSINE2 (longitudinal absolute-abundance task), no foundation pretraining, no perturbation testing, and no final project claim. The direct old AGP mirror overlap is a separate holdout risk, not a source in this pilot.

Sources: old https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metagenomics/api/v1/studies/MGYS00002394/pipelines/4.1/file/SRP051741_taxonomy_abundances_SSU_v4.1.tsv ; new https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metagenomics/api/v1/studies/MGYS00002146/pipelines/4.1/file/SRP009299_taxonomy_abundances_SSU_v4.1.tsv ; new per-sample metadata https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metagenomics/api/v1/studies/MGYS00002146/samples?page_size=100 .

**Metric scope correction:** the primary pilot errors compare compositions after test taxa are projected onto the 160 train genera and renormalized. Thus they are a *restricted-vocabulary* task, not whole-community error. Median test count mass in vocabulary 99.58%, minimum 89.73%. One site falls below a later proposed 90% coverage threshold. Secondary sensitivity in `pilot_oral_sensitivity.json` gives zero predicted mass to all unseen test genera and scores against full 339-genus truth. Full-community person-macro BC: presence-mean 0.58591, GraphTwin 0.58763, cNODE 0.59276, gLV 0.60833, transformer 0.62063. Excluding one low-coverage site yields 57 people and the same numeric order (prior 0.58610, GraphTwin 0.58770, cNODE 0.59296, gLV 0.60848, transformer 0.62037). These are post-pilot sensitivity analyses, not preregistered primary tests or evidence of a general top-tool rank.
