# Oral-to-oral one-study pilot, 2026-09-26

This is a debug transfer pilot under `PILOT_TRANSFER_PROTOCOL.md`, **not** the requested 500-1000-dataset leaderboard or a top-tool beat. All models were trained on the 20-site old MGnify oral accession MGYS00002394 and predicted the disjoint accession MGYS00002146; its 178 runs pooled to 87 sites from 58 people (29 controls; 29 diseased with two sites). All use MGnify v4.1 tables and train-vocabulary genus projection. Zero projected-mass sites excluded: 0. Full per-site and per-person errors, source SHA-256s and protocol hash are in `pilot_oral_transfer.json`. No model was tuned on the candidate study.

Primary descriptive mean across people of each person's median site Bray-Curtis: population presence-mean **0.58346**; GraphTwin 0.58514; cNODE reimplementation 0.59091; gLV 0.60632; experimental transformer 0.61802. Thus the simple prior is numerically best in this *single study* and none of the tested interaction models establishes a win here. Per-site medians differ; see JSON. The 20 train samples and 58 test people are too small/narrow for a global model-choice rule, and a one-study test has no valid study-level interval. Biological study and preprocessing differences can explain errors; the model does not show causal dependence. No MDSINE2 (longitudinal absolute-abundance task), no foundation pretraining, no perturbation testing, and no final project claim. The direct old AGP mirror overlap is a separate holdout risk, not a source in this pilot.

Sources: old https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metagenomics/api/v1/studies/MGYS00002394/pipelines/4.1/file/SRP051741_taxonomy_abundances_SSU_v4.1.tsv ; new https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metagenomics/api/v1/studies/MGYS00002146/pipelines/4.1/file/SRP009299_taxonomy_abundances_SSU_v4.1.tsv ; new per-sample metadata https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metagenomics/api/v1/studies/MGYS00002146/samples?page_size=100 .

**Person-unit sensitivity, corrected 22:55 IST:** The locked `macro_person_median_bray_curtis` statistic is the **mean across people of each person's site-median error**, not the median across people. The first version of `scripts/oral_pilot_person_sensitivity.py` resampled the latter and labeled it as the former; its intervals are withdrawn for the locked metric. The corrected script resamples paired people, averages their site-median model-minus-prior differences and verifies that the unresampled means reproduce the locked pilot point results. Correct locked-statistic differences, model minus prior, with 10,000 person-bootstrap percentile intervals: cNODE +0.00745 [-0.00526,+0.02024]; GraphTwin +0.00168 [-0.00246,+0.00583]; gLV +0.02285 [+0.01414,+0.03235]; experimental transformer +0.03456 [+0.01980,+0.04969]. On this viewed one-study pilot cNODE and GraphTwin remain indistinguishable from the simple prior at this person-resampling level, while gLV and transformer are descriptively worse. A post-hoc median-across-people delta is retained in the JSON, labeled as a **different estimand**, and is not used as locked-protocol evidence. One study cannot give a study-level interval or final top-tool verdict; model multiplicity remains unadjusted. Full source JSON hash and arithmetic are in `results/oral_pilot_person_sensitivity.json`.

**Predictive gain on the viewed study only, 22:39 IST:** `scripts/oral_pilot_dependence.py` applies the signed fractional error-reduction definition to paired **person-median** model/prior errors. GraphTwin's median is -0.00268, cNODE -0.01408, gLV -0.03365 and transformer -0.05321: on this task none shows positive median gain over the presence prior. This is not a causal interaction-necessity score. All 58 people belong to the same test study, so the study-group interval is explicitly unavailable rather than incorrectly bootstrapping 58 independent studies. These are post-pilot diagnostics, not a final external leaderboard or a claim about unseen patient outcomes.

**Historical protocol wording note:** The hash-pinned `PILOT_TRANSFER_PROTOCOL.md` is immutable as the original pre-result artifact. Its phrase "median Bray-Curtis by each person (sites aggregated within person)" is underspecified about aggregation **across people**. The executed script and locked result JSON use the arithmetic mean across people of their within-person site medians. This README clarifies the executed estimand without rewriting and invalidating the original protocol hash. The distinct mean-vs-median interpretation is an audit caveat; no prospective final benchmark claim rests on this pilot.

**Paired-site error diagnostic (post-hoc, 27 September):** `scripts/oral_paired_site_diagnostic.py` takes the pinned site-error vectors and source-derived person map, pairs healthy-site H and diseased-site D observations within each of the 29 diseased people, and resamples whole paired people. In the *same viewed study*, mean within-person D-minus-H error is -0.004728 for the population prior, -0.025926 cNODE, +0.001611 gLV, +0.004095 GraphTwin and -0.006300 transformer. All five 95% person-bootstrap intervals include zero. The interaction contrast (a model's D-minus-H gap minus the prior's gap) also includes zero for all four challengers; see `results/oral_paired_site_diagnostic.json` for full intervals and exact input hashes. This exploratory paired-site contrast is not an intervention, a replicated biological discovery, evidence for causal interaction necessity, or an untouched-source validation. Comparisons across models were not multiplicity-adjusted.
